## 2. Transaction

Acquisition of MSK a.d. Kikinda from the Republic of Serbia / Srbijagas, followed by a modernisation and restart programme returning the complex to production of approximately 200,000 t/y methanol and 100,000 t/y acetic acid, with a feedstock strategy allowing natural gas, agricultural biomass and prepared non-hazardous waste-derived feedstock.

**Acquisition price** is payable by the Founder under a deferred seller credit at HoldCo level and is **not** funded from the Investor's capital or from ProjectCo's modernisation budget.

### Reserved matters (Investor consent required)

Issue of new shares or convertible instruments; amendment of constitutional documents affecting Investor rights; related-party transactions above €250,000; incurrence of debt taking net debt above 3.5x EBITDA; grant of security outside the agreed financing; disposals above €5m; winding up, insolvency filing or material change of business; distributions outside the agreed waterfall; appointment or removal of the auditor; approval of the annual budget.

**Budget deemed-approval mechanic.** If no annual budget is approved before the start of a financial year, the prior year's budget applies, uplifted by CPI, until a new budget is approved. Capex within an approved budget requires no further consent; capex outside it requires consent only above €5m.

**No Investor veto** over refinancing, over ordinary commercial contracts, or over exercise of the Founder Call.

## 7. Distribution waterfall

1. Operating costs and maintenance capex
2. Taxes
3. Senior debt interest
4. Senior debt scheduled principal
5. Debt service reserve top-up to 6 months
6. Maintenance reserve top-up
7. Shareholder loan interest (cash-pay subject to DSCR ≥1.20x)
8. Shareholder loan principal
9. Ordinary distributions pro rata, retaining a 10% cash cushion

The Founder's acquisition obligation is serviced **exclusively from the Founder's share of distributions at step 9**, outside ProjectCo.

Senior cash sweep, if required by lenders: 50% of excess cash above 3.0x net debt / EBITDA; nil below.

## 9. Cost overruns

Funded strictly in the following order; each layer exhausted before the next:

1. The 10% contingency reserve within the capex budget
2. EPC contractor liability under the lump-sum turnkey wrap, supported by a performance bond equal to 10% of contract value and liquidated damages for delay and performance shortfall
3. A committed standby senior / ECA tranche of €20m, undrawn at close
4. Subordinated overrun loans offered to **both** shareholders **pro rata**, at the shareholder loan coupon plus 200bp
5. If the Founder does not fund its pro rata share, the Investor may fund 100% of that tranche and receive a preferential return **on that tranche only**
6. Issue of new equity, **only** at fair market value determined by an independent internationally recognised valuer, as a last resort

**No automatic conversion of overrun funding into equity. No equity cure priced at a discount to fair market value. No Founder personal guarantee and no recourse to any Founder asset outside ProjectCo.**

## 12. Conditions precedent

1. Satisfactory independent **technical due diligence** confirming that the modernisation programme delivers the agreed production and operating cost targets
2. Satisfactory independent **market study** on methanol and acetic acid, and on feedstock availability and cost
3. **Phase I and Phase II environmental assessment**, and a binding allocation of all pre-closing environmental liability to the State with a statutory or contractual carve-out in favour of ProjectCo
4. Legacy indebtedness of MSK, including amounts owed to Srbijagas, **written off, converted or assumed by the seller** prior to closing
5. Executed share purchase agreement with the Republic of Serbia / Srbijagas, including agreed investment and employment obligations
6. Committed senior debt and, where applicable, **ECA cover** on agreed terms
7. Executed EPC contract on a lump-sum turnkey basis for the wrapped scope, with the agreed bonding
8. Feedstock supply and product offtake arrangements covering at least 50% of planned output
9. All merger control, foreign investment and regulatory approvals
10. Agreed shareholders' agreement, articles, and management incentive plan
